# Onboarding

Welcome to the team! Your first week is mostly setup and meeting people.

## Day 1

- Pick up you laptop from IT on the 3rd floor.
- Log in to Slack and join #general and you're team channel.
- Your manager will scheduel a 1:1 for the afternoon.

## Day 2-5

- Read the architecture overveiw in the wiki.
- Shadow a on-call shift with your buddy.
- Ship a small first change; your buddy will help you find one.
