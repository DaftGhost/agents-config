## Claude Code: Eight Dos and Don'ts

- Look up APIs; don't guess.
- When in doubt, ask; don't forge ahead.
- Confirm business rules with a human; don't make them up.
- Reuse existing interfaces; don't reinvent them.
- Test your work; don't skip verification.
- Follow project conventions; don't break the architecture.
- Admit what you don't know; don't pretend to understand.
- Refactor with care; don't edit blindly.
